RADD / OverlapText — type over image, contrast-safe

Type sits on top,
and stays legible.
Rule: display type never sits on raw image pixels. Every overlap uses one of three sanctioned treatments — (a) gradient scrim behind the text's bounding box, (b) a solid color-block panel, or (c) weight ≥700 plus hard shadow clearing 3:1 against worst-case pixels. Overlaps live in the z-type layer (20) over z-overlap (10).
